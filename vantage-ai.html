01Overview

About Vantage AI

I first built Vantage AI to automate feature comparisons. I added citations, editable ratings, and roadmap priorities so users could review the research and choose what to investigate next.

02Problem

Why I built it

Competitive research often means moving between product websites, spreadsheets, and presentations. Gathering information is only part of the work: someone still needs to check it, compare features consistently, and decide what matters.

A / Gather

Scattered information

Documentation, pricing, and feature pages describe different plans and product scopes.

B / Compare

Unsupported claims

AI-generated comparisons can look complete even when evidence is missing.

C / Decide

Unclear priorities

A competitor’s feature is not enough reason to build it.

03Strategy

Intended users and priorities

I chose these priorities from my own workflow and implementation review, expecting fewer steps to a useful comparison. Intended users and goals remain untested.

Product managers
Compare products and identify gaps worth investigating.
Founders
Understand competitors and choose where to focus.
Consultants
Prepare a comparison for a client recommendation.
Analysts
Check sources, correct ratings, and export findings.

What I prioritized

  1. Reliable research. Link claims to sources and prevent stale results.
  2. Editable ratings. Allow corrections without losing earlier work.
  3. Useful outputs. Rank gaps and export consistent results.

Planned evaluation

  • Time to a reviewed comparison versus a matched manual task.
  • Citation accuracy and understanding of uncertain ratings.
  • Task completion, successful corrections, and usefulness.
  • Consistency across the dashboard and exports.

01 / Research

Sources and editable ratings

Gemini proposes five comparison criteria. Google Search grounding links sources to individual assessments where available, alongside explanations, dates, and qualitative confidence. Unsupported claims remain Unverified. Users can edit ratings and add notes while retaining the originals.

YesPartialNoUnverified
02 / Compare

Weighted scores and charts

Adjustable importance weights drive the scores and bar chart; the radar chart shows feature profiles. Unverified ratings are excluded from the known-feature score. Evidence coverage and score bounds show how much remains unknown, and edits recalculate the results immediately.

03 / Prioritize

A ranked product roadmap

Users estimate impact and effort to rank gaps. Each item includes an explanation and next action; missing evidence prompts further research. Comparison CSV, roadmap CSV, and executive PDF exports use the dashboard’s current calculations.

Priority = competitive gap × importance × impact ÷ effort

Gap compares your rating with the strongest assessed competitor. Importance ranges from 1–10; impact and effort are relative 1–5 estimates, not measured customer value or business returns.

06Decisions

Key product decisions

Select a decision to read more.

Decision 01Leave unsupported claims unverified
Problem
A filled-in matrix can hide weak evidence.
Options
Use model knowledge, broad page citations, or claim-level sources.
Choice
I required supporting sources and marked other claims Unverified.
Why
Missing evidence does not establish that a feature is absent.
Trade-off
Results may be incomplete. Reviewers still need to check sources, product identity, and plan scope.
Decision 02Let users correct the research
Problem
AI ratings can be incomplete or wrong.
Options
Rerun the request, lock the results, or allow direct edits.
Choice
I added editable ratings and notes, retaining originals and recalculating dependent results.
Why
Corrections should survive weight changes and be easy to review.
Trade-off
Edits are labeled user judgments. Work must be exported before leaving the session.
Decision 03Keep prioritization simple
Problem
A gap list gives little guidance; detailed revenue forecasts would lack supporting data.
Options
Leave gaps unranked, estimate reach and revenue, or use impact and effort.
Choice
I used editable importance, impact, and effort estimates and kept Streamlit.
Why
The calculation is easy to review, and the existing Python stack supports the workflow.
Trade-off
Scores guide discussion, not validated ROI. Accounts and shared workspaces remain deferred.
