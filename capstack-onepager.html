CapStack

Compare two ways to allocate the same contribution across retirement, brokerage, and savings accounts—with visible assumptions and no asset custody.

$12 / mo or $99/year · proposed

01Problem & opportunity

User and decision

An active investor aged roughly 22–35 has several account types and a bonus, payroll change, or savings goal to plan for. The useful question is how two alternatives change the same goal—not merely what the combined balance is.

Competitive position

Empower already offers linked accounts and retirement scenarios. Boldin offers linked accounts and side-by-side planning at $144/year for PlannerPlus. [C1–C3]

CapStack must win a narrower accumulation-stage task through less setup and clearer tradeoffs.

02Product approach

Core product loop

Confirm account inputs → define the contribution and goal → review assumptions → compare alternatives → save the decision.

Side-by-side comparison belongs in V1. Manual entry must offer the same calculation quality as connected accounts.

Scope and release gates

P0: manual and eligible linked accounts, validated calculations, contribution checks, comparison, and reproducible saved scenarios. Goals and richer history follow.

Defer ranked recommendations, opaque tax scores, and detailed tax optimization pending model and legal review. Not holding assets or displaying a disclaimer does not settle regulatory status. [C7]

03Requirements

Model and acceptance criteria

Use a versioned monthly model with explicit contribution timing, returns, fees, and inflation. Compare alternatives under common assumptions. Separate principal from growth and accessible funds from retirement balances.

Reference test: $10,000 at zero return plus twelve $500 deposits = $16,000.
Invariant: the same inputs and model version produce the same output. Saved scenarios retain their original assumptions.

Data and rule handling

Show account-level timestamps and missing inputs. Never treat unknown contribution history as zero or double-count manual and linked versions of an account. Plaid integration availability is verified by provider. [C4]

Use reviewed tax-year rules. For 2026, base limits include $24,500 employee 401(k) deferrals and $7,500 IRA contributions; eligibility and catch-up treatment require separate handling. [C5, C6]

04Validation

Validation and metrics

First, test 12–15 users on an actual decision and compare against their current workflow. Then propose a 50-user, six-week beta.

Gates: ≥80% comprehension, ≥65% activation, zero known critical calculation defects. Retain >40% D30 retention and 3+ monthly scenarios as targets, with clear cohort definitions.

Measure whether a comparison informs a decision; extra runs may reflect confusion.

05Economics

Pricing and economics

At 10,000 paid users, annualized revenue ranges from $990K if all pay $99 annually to $1.44M if all pay $12 monthly. A 50/50 mix yields $1.215M.

The original $10K MRR target needs about 834 monthly subscribers. A 500-user beta at 25% conversion yields $1,500 MRR. Account aggregation, support, and acquisition must fit the margin.

06Next decisions

What would change the decision

If the task is too infrequent for a subscription, test different packaging. If linking dominates support, narrow provider coverage. If users cannot explain the assumptions, simplify the model before adding features.

The core bet is an understandable, repeatable cross-account decision—not the claim that other planning tools cannot exist.
