CapStack

Compare how two account allocations could affect the same savings goal.

Product concept · Not launched or validated.

01Problem

Planning across accounts

Investors aged 22–35 may need to allocate a bonus, changed paycheck, or savings contribution across accounts. A combined balance does not answer where new money should go.

Existing alternatives

Empower offers linked accounts and retirement scenarios. Boldin includes side-by-side planning at $144/year for PlannerPlus. [C1–C3] CapStack would focus on simpler setup and clearer comparisons for investors building savings.

02Proposed product

Workflow: confirm accounts → set contribution and goal → review assumptions → compare → save.

I prioritized manual and eligible linked accounts with matching calculations, contribution checks, comparisons, and reproducible saved scenarios. Goals and richer history would follow.

Ranked recommendations, opaque tax scores, and detailed tax optimization need model and legal review. Avoiding asset custody alone does not resolve regulatory status. [C7]

03Calculations & data

Consistent comparisons

A monthly model would compare alternatives under shared contribution timing, return, fee, and inflation assumptions, separating principal from growth and accessible funds from retirement balances. Saved scenarios retain inputs and model versions.

Reference test: $10,000 at zero return plus twelve $500 deposits = $16,000. Identical inputs and versions must reproduce results.

Account rules

Show timestamps and missing inputs; avoid treating unknown contributions as zero or double-counting linked and manual accounts. Check Plaid availability by provider. [C4]

For 2026, base limits are $24,500 for employee 401(k) deferrals and $7,500 for IRA contributions; eligibility and catch-up rules need separate handling. [C5, C6]

04Testing plan

Proposed tests: compare current workflows with 12–15 users, then run a 50-user, six-week beta.

Targets: ≥80% comprehension, ≥65% activation, zero known critical calculation defects, >40% D30 retention, and 3+ monthly scenarios. Define cohorts and check whether comparisons inform decisions; extra runs may indicate confusion.

05Business model

Proposed pricing: $12/month or $99/year. At 10,000 paid users, annualized revenue would be $990K at annual pricing, $1.44M at monthly pricing, or $1.215M at a 50/50 mix.

The $10K MRR target needs about 834 monthly subscribers. A separate 500-user beta scenario at 25% conversion yields $1,500 MRR. Margins must cover aggregation, support, and acquisition.

06Next steps

Test clarity and willingness to pay. Adjust packaging for infrequent use, narrow providers if linking drives support, and simplify assumptions users cannot explain.
