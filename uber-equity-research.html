01Overview

I built an operating forecast through FY2030, an unlevered DCF, and a peer comparison from financial statements. Bear, base, and bull cases vary growth, margins, discount rates, and acquisition outcomes.

Reported results and forecast assumptions are identified separately. The DCF uses unlevered cash flow, distinct from company-reported free cash flow. Delivery Hero remains a proposed acquisition in this analysis.

02Investment thesis

Mobility and Delivery economics

Higher trip volume, delivery density, and engagement could improve margins and cash flow. I modeled gross bookings alongside revenue and GAAP operating margins to assess demand and profitability.

UK Mobility business-model changes affect recognized revenue, so revenue divided by bookings is not a pure take rate. Changing segment-profit definitions also limit historical comparisons.

Autonomous vehicles: opportunity and risk

Uber could connect riders with autonomous fleets through its network and partnerships. Risks include robotaxi operators bypassing Uber and fleet investment exceeding the standalone forecast.

Catalysts to watch

Bookings growth, sustained margin gains, and profitable AV partnerships would strengthen the thesis. The October analysis flags Q3 earnings on 4 November 2026 and the announced Delivery Hero offer deadline on 5 November for updates on performance, financing, and deal risk.

03DCF & scenarios

The DCF adds depreciation and amortization to after-tax operating profit, then deducts capex and incremental working capital. Equity value adjusts for debt, unrestricted cash, financial investments, and a 2.5% share dilution cushion.

Base assumptions: 10.0% WACC and 2.75% terminal growth. With 78.5% of standalone enterprise value from terminal value, long-term assumptions strongly affect the result.

05Risks & decision

  • Economics: Insurance costs, incentives, regulation, and weaker unit returns could limit margins despite trip growth.
  • Capital allocation: Acquisition financing and autonomous-fleet investment could raise leverage and reduce cash returns.
  • Valuation: Discount rates, terminal growth, investment values, and dilution materially affect valuation.

A lower entry price, sustained profit growth, and clearer acquisition and AV economics would prompt me to revisit the recommendation.
