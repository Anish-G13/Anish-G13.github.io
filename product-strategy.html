SiteFlow

Mobile-first workflow automation for Clinical Research Coordinators

SiteFlow is a proposed mobile workflow for coordinators at small independent clinical research sites. It focuses on visit documentation and investigator review. Existing tools such as Veeva SiteVault already serve sites; the case study tests whether a narrower workflow can reduce total administrative time enough to justify a paid product.

Market input 9,700 sites · original planning estimate
Proposed pricing $199 / mo per site
ARR scenario @ 5% adoption $1.16M
Pilot goal ≥30% less administrative time per visit, without worse completeness

CapStack

Mobile-first cross-account scenario planning for active retail investors

CapStack is a proposed cross-account planning tool for active investors aged roughly 22–35. It compares two ways to allocate the same contribution across existing accounts, with visible assumptions. Empower and Boldin already offer planning and linked accounts; the opportunity is a faster, clearer contribution decision.

Market input 52M investors · original research estimate
Proposed pricing $12 / mo Pro tier
ARR scenario @ 10K monthly subscribers $1.44M
Pilot goal ≥80% comprehension and ≥65% comparison activation

Exhibit D · AI Productivity 04 / 04

DraftDesk

Source-linked QBRs and escalation summaries for customer success teams

DraftDesk is a proposed drafting workflow for customer success managers producing QBRs and escalation summaries. It combines permitted account context with source-linked facts and human review. Microsoft and Notion already connect business context; the case study focuses on reducing preparation and review time for these specific documents.

Market input 70M+ US knowledge workers · broad research estimate
Proposed pricing $18 / seat Pro · $14 / seat Team
ARR scenario @ 7,500 Pro seats $1.62M
Pilot goal ≥30% less preparation and review time at equal or better quality
