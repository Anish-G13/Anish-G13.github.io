Product concept · Healthtech 02 / 04

SiteFlow

Visit documentation for clinical research coordinators

A mobile app concept for documenting clinical research visits and sending reports for investigator review. I focused on small independent sites and existing tools such as Veeva SiteVault.

Market estimate 9,700 sites
Proposed pricing $199 / mo per site
ARR scenario @ 5% adoption $1.16M
Pilot goal ≥30% less administrative time per visit, without reducing completeness

CapStack

Investment planning across multiple accounts

A planning app concept for comparing two ways to invest the same contribution across existing accounts. I considered active investors aged 22–35 and alternatives such as Empower and Boldin.

Market estimate 52M investors
Proposed pricing $12 / mo Pro tier
ARR scenario @ 10K monthly subscribers $1.44M
Pilot goal ≥80% comprehension and ≥65% comparison activation

Product concept · AI Productivity 04 / 04

DraftDesk

Quarterly reviews and escalation summaries for customer success teams

An AI writing tool concept for customer success teams preparing quarterly reviews and escalation summaries. Drafts would cite account records and require human review. I compared the idea with Microsoft and Notion.

Market estimate 70M+ US knowledge workers
Proposed pricing $18 / seat Pro · $14 / seat Team
ARR scenario @ 7,500 Pro seats $1.62M
Pilot goal ≥30% less preparation and review time at equal or better quality
