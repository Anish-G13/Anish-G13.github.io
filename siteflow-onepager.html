SiteFlow

Mobile workflow automation for clinical research coordinators

A focused visit-to-review workflow for small independent research sites.

01Problem & opportunity

Problem and customer

A coordinator at a site running 1–10 trials must reconcile visit work across clinical records, sponsor systems, and regulatory documents. Original discovery identified repeated concerns about administrative burden, fragmented systems, and delayed signatures. The initial buyer is the investigator or site director; privacy, sponsor, and institutional approval may also be necessary.

Competitive position

Veeva SiteVault already provides site-focused eISF and related workflows; its eISF tier is free through 20 active studies. Florence offers eRegulatory, eSource, and eConsent. [S1–S3]

SiteFlow must prove a faster, easier visit-to-review task for the chosen practice. A paid product must justify itself against a free alternative. Sponsor EDC integration and multi-site administration are deferred.

02Product approach

Product decision

Start with one study and one approved visit workflow. A coordinator captures a checklist, reviews a prepared documentation packet, and routes the exact record version for investigator review. Measure the whole process, including rework and external EDC entry, before claiming a productivity gain.

V1 scope and user flow

1 · Mobile intake
Study-specific fields, save state, and template versions. Offline changes remain pending until reconciled.

2 · Documentation preparation
Prepare source-linked entries for review. Unsupported events become exception tasks, not generated compliance claims.

3 · Investigator review
Authorized reviewer, exact record version, and retained signature history. Material changes require renewed review.

03Requirements

Acceptance criteria that matter

No silent overwrites when two devices edit a record. Signed versions stay immutable. Missing required items block completion. Exports preserve relevant metadata and history. Offline signing is blocked until server reconciliation.

FDA guidance makes validation and record integrity part of the implementation. Real-data pilots require applicable privacy agreements and approved procedures. [S4–S6]

04Validation

Pilot and success measures

Proposed study: six sites, two baseline weeks, four pilot weeks, with comparable visit types.

Primary gate: ≥30% lower median administrative time without worse completeness. Measure review delay, correction rates, and support effort.

Retain the original ≥4 weekly active CRC sessions/site target; test paid continuation with at least three of six pilot sites. Targets are not pilot results.

05Economics

Economics and acquisition

9,700 × 5% × $199 × 12 = $1,158,180 annualized revenue. The site count and adoption rate are scenario inputs.

At an illustrative $40/hour, the subscription requires about five hours of monthly savings before onboarding costs. Recruit coordinator champions and test the buyer’s actual approval process; do not assume $199 bypasses procurement.

06Next decisions

What would change the decision

Narrow or stop if benefits disappear after sponsor-system reconciliation, if each protocol needs extensive custom engineering, or if the buyer prefers an existing free tool after a fair task comparison.

The next evidence should be observed workflow results, a tested record-integrity implementation, and paid-pilot decisions.
