SiteFlow

A mobile tool for preparing and reviewing clinical visit records.

Product concept · Not launched or validated.

Product planning & PRDRead the full PRD ↓

01Problem

Coordinators and site directors

Sites running 1–10 trials juggle clinical records, sponsor systems, and regulatory documents. The concept research reviewed 53 sources on administrative work, fragmented systems, and delayed signatures. The buyer would be an investigator or site director, with privacy, sponsor, and institutional approvals as needed.

Existing alternatives

Veeva SiteVault offers site workflows and free eISF for up to 20 active studies. Florence offers eRegulatory, eSource, and eConsent. [S1–S3] SiteFlow would need to make visit review faster and simpler to justify paying.

02Proposed product

One study, one visit workflow

I scoped the first version around three steps, deferring sponsor EDC integration and multi-site administration.

1 · Mobile intake
Study-specific fields, saved progress, and template versions; offline edits wait for reconciliation.

2 · Documentation
Prepare source-linked entries; flag unsupported events for follow-up.

3 · Investigator review
Route the exact version to an authorized reviewer and retain signature history. Material edits require renewed review.

03Requirements

Prevent silent overwrites, keep signed versions immutable, and block completion when required items are missing. Exports retain metadata and history; offline signing waits for server reconciliation.

FDA guidance informs validation and record integrity. Real-data pilots need applicable privacy agreements and approved procedures. [S4–S6]

04Testing plan

Proposed pilot: six sites, two baseline weeks, four pilot weeks, and comparable visit types.

Targets: ≥30% less median administrative time without lower completeness; ≥4 weekly active CRC sessions/site; paid continuation from at least three of six sites.

Include rework and external EDC entry in timing. Track review delays, corrections, and support effort.

05Business model

Revenue scenario: 9,700 sites × 5% adoption × $199/month × 12 = $1,158,180 annualized.

At an assumed $40/hour, the price requires about five hours of monthly savings before onboarding. Recruit coordinator champions and test buyer approvals.

06Next steps

Observe workflows, test record integrity, and seek paid-pilot decisions. Narrow or stop if sponsor-system reconciliation erases savings, protocols need extensive custom work, or sites prefer an existing free tool.
