DraftDesk

Prepare quarterly business reviews and escalation summaries from account records.

Product concept · Not launched or validated.

Product planning & PRDRead the full PRD ↓

01Problem

Recurring customer documents

Customer success managers gather account data, call notes, and internal knowledge before writing. The intended user manages 40 accounts and prepares 8–12 documents monthly; a team lead is the proposed buyer.

Existing alternatives

Microsoft offers CRM-aware summaries and meeting preparation with Salesforce support. Notion added Salesforce search in March 2026. [D1–D3] DraftDesk would need to reduce total preparation and review time beyond these tools.

02Proposed product

I scoped two documents: QBRs and escalation summaries. Choose account/template → collect permitted sources → confirm context → draft → review/edit → export.

QBRs need supported progress and next actions; escalations need accurate chronology and commitments. RFP responses and marketing briefs would follow after source handling and quality checks pass.

03Requirements

Access and accuracy

Gong OAuth requires organization-level tech-admin approval; individual call permissions still apply. [D4, D5]

Map Salesforce fields, preserve record, object, and field permissions, and flag missing context. [D6]

Figures, dates, outcomes, and commitments need sources or user confirmation. Resolve conflicting sources, preserve accepted edits in version history, and export the reviewed version intact. Downloads do not count as customer sends.

04Testing plan

Quality checks

Proposed suite: 40 approved or simulated QBR and escalation packets with stale data, contradictions, missing fields, wrong accounts, and malicious transcript instructions.

Targets: 100% fidelity for critical numbers and dates; ≥95% supported claims; zero critical unsupported claims or unauthorized disclosures.

Proposed five-team pilot

Compare matched tasks against current workflows with blind quality reviews.

Targets: ≥30% less human preparation and review time at equal or better quality; ≥70% acceptance with fewer than two substantive revision rounds; 3+ weekly drafts, segmented by document cadence; p95 generation latency ≤60 seconds.

05Business model

Proposed plans: Free, 5 drafts/month; Pro, $18/seat/month; Team, $14/seat/month for 5+ seats.

Revenue scenarios: 7,500 seats yield $1.62M annualized at Pro pricing, $1.26M at Team pricing, or $1.44M at a 50/50 mix.

At $14 and a 75% contribution margin, variable costs must stay ≤$3.50/seat/month. Estimate generation, connector, and support costs before offering unlimited use.

06Next steps

Proposed 13-week plan

Weeks 1–2: observe work, recruit partners, define permissions and evaluation.
Weeks 3–5: two templates, manual context, source review, versioned editing.
Weeks 6–8: approved connectors and DOCX export.
Weeks 9–11: pilot and paired timing.
Weeks 12–13: fix issues and test paid continuation.

When to reconsider

Stop affected retrieval after permission failures. Reconsider if fact-checking erases savings or existing Microsoft, Notion, or Gong tools perform equally well. Before expanding, I would check draft accuracy, account permissions, time savings, and whether pilot teams would pay.
